Laboratory Test Details

Test Name: Complete Blood Count (CBC)

Purpose: To check overall health and detect infections.

Sample Required: Blood Sample

Preparation Instructions: No special preparation is required.

Normal Reference Range: As advised by the laboratory.

Report Delivery Time: Within 24 Hours

Important Instruction: Carry your doctor's prescription.

Fasting Requirement: Fasting is required for some blood tests.

For more information, contact the laboratory.
